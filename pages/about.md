# About Me

Hi! I'm **{{ site.author.name }}**, an Information Sciences + Data Science student at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign**, with an expected graduation in Spring 2029.

I'm passionate about exploring how emerging technologies can solve real-world problems, particularly at the intersection of **data science, artificial intelligence, software engineering, and quantum computing**.

## My Background

Through my coursework, independent projects, and extracurricular activities, I've developed experience in programming, data analysis, machine learning, and full-stack web development.

I enjoy building projects that go beyond theoretical concepts and turn ideas into practical, interactive applications. Some of my work includes:

- **TripPilot:** A full-stack travel planning platform featuring AI-generated itineraries, flight and hotel price monitoring, automated alerts, and persistent trip management.
- **Quantum Pulse:** A quantum computing industry analytics project that uses Python-based data processing and interactive dashboards to explore quantum hardware, technology trends, and industry developments.
- **NEXUS:** An interactive space exploration application featuring 3D visualizations, immersive navigation, and a foundation for incorporating real astronomical datasets and machine learning.

These projects have helped me strengthen my technical skills while developing a deeper understanding of how data and software can work together.

## My Interests and Goals

I'm especially interested in the future of **quantum computing** and its potential applications in artificial intelligence, optimization, and scientific discovery.

My long-term goal is to contribute to research and development in quantum technologies, particularly quantum algorithms and quantum machine learning.

I'm also interested in opportunities involving data analytics, machine learning, cybersecurity, and software development, where I can apply my current skills while continuing to learn.

## Leadership and Involvement

Outside of academics, I value leadership, mentorship, and collaboration.

I'm involved with the **Illinois Leadership Center** as a Brand Ambassador and serve as the **Public & Member Relations Chair for the Illini Tang Soo Do Club**.

My background in martial arts instruction and student leadership has helped me develop communication, teamwork, organization, and mentoring skills.
