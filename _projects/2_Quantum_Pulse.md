# Quantum Pulse

**Category:** Data Science, Data Analytics, Data Visualization

**Status:** In Development

## Overview

Quantum Pulse is a data analytics project focused on exploring the rapidly evolving quantum computing industry through structured datasets and visualizations.

The project organizes information about quantum computing companies, hardware systems, technological approaches, performance metrics, and major industry events.

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Tableau
- Google Looker Studio
- CSV-based data pipelines

## Key Features

### Quantum Hardware Dataset

Organizes information about quantum computing companies, their hardware systems, and the technologies used to develop them.

### Hardware Metrics

Tracks reported quantum hardware characteristics, including physical qubit counts, programmable qubit counts, and other performance indicators.

### Industry Event Tracking

Maintains structured information about quantum computing developments and their relationships to companies.

### Data Processing

Uses Python and Pandas to organize, clean, and transform datasets for analysis and visualization.

## Dataset Development

At a recorded development milestone, the dataset included:

- 12 companies
- 6 technology categories
- 19 quantum systems
- 32 hardware metrics
- 39 industry events

## Future Development

I plan to continue expanding the dataset and improving interactive visualizations to make comparisons between quantum computing technologies more accessible.
